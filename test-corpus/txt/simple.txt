Chapter 1

The sun rose over the quiet village. Birds sang in the trees, and the air smelled of fresh bread. Elena walked down the cobblestone street, her boots clicking against the stones.

She paused at the bakery window, watching the baker shape loaves with practiced hands. The scent of yeast and wheat wrapped around her like a warm blanket.

"Good morning," she said, smiling at the baker.

"Good morning," he replied. "The usual?"

"The usual, please."

Chapter 2

Later that afternoon, Elena sat by the river. The water flowed gently past mossy rocks, and dragonflies darted above the surface. She opened her book and began to read, losing herself in the story.

The world around her faded away. There was only the rustle of pages, the murmur of the river, and the quiet peace of a summer day.
